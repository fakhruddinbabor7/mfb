Research

Secure and intelligent cyber-physical systems under real-time constraints

My research focuses on securing real-time, embedded, and cyber-physical systems. I study timing-based information leakage, randomized scheduling and control stability, confidential AI execution, and learning-based vulnerability prioritization.

Research direction

Build systems that remain secure, predictable, and useful when timing, resource limits, privacy, and control requirements all interact.
